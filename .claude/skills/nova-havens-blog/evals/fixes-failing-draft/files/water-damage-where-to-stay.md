> When water damage makes your home unsafe or unusable, you usually have three places to go: family or friends, a hotel, or a furnished temporary home arranged for the length of the repairs. Drying and mitigation alone commonly take one to three weeks before rebuilding starts, so plan for longer than the first estimate. Nova Havens Temporary Housing coordinates furnished temporary housing across the 48 contiguous US states and can be reached at (629) 401-0054.

Finding water in the kitchen at six in the morning is a particular kind of bad day. The good news is that water damage, unlike fire, usually leaves most of the house standing, and the housing question is about weeks rather than months. This guide walks through the decision in the order it tends to arrive.

## The first 24 hours

Stop the water, then document it. Shut the main valve if the source is a pipe, and switch off power to any room where water has reached outlets. Photograph every affected room before anything is moved, including the water line on the walls and the underside of cabinets. Call your insurer's claims line the same day and tell them the house is not habitable; ask for the claim number and write it down.

Your policy covers the cost of staying elsewhere under Additional Living Expense, so keep every receipt from the first night onward. Hotels, meals above your normal spending, laundry and mileage are all typically reimbursed, usually for as long as the repairs take.

## How long will you be out

It depends on how far the water travelled and how long it sat. According to Ready.gov, water that has been standing for more than 48 hours should be treated as a mold risk, which extends the drying phase. A mitigation company will usually run dehumidifiers and air movers for three to seven days, then a contractor assesses what has to be replaced. Flooring, drywall below the water line and cabinet bases are the usual casualties.

A realistic range for a single-room incident is two to four weeks. A whole-floor incident, or any incident involving sewage or category 3 water, is commonly six to twelve weeks. The Insurance Information Institute notes that water damage is the second most common home insurance claim in the United States, so contractors in most metros have seen your situation many times before.

## Your three housing options

**Family or friends.** Free, familiar and fine for a few nights. Hard on everyone for three weeks, and harder with children, pets or anyone who works from home.

**A hotel.** Available tonight, and usually the right call for the first two or three nights while you find out how long you will be out. Hotels are not built for weeks: no kitchen, one room, and a nightly rate that adds up fast.

**A furnished temporary home.** A whole house or apartment, already furnished, utilities on, arranged for a set term that runs alongside the repairs. With 60,000+ properties in our network, we can usually place a family within 24 to 48 hours of the first call. This is the option built for the weeks between the hotel and your own front door.

Be honest about duration. A home arranged for two weeks and a home arranged for two months are different homes, and an optimistic estimate usually means a second move.

## Mold, and why it changes the timeline

If drying starts late, mold can begin within 48 to 72 hours of the water event, and remediation adds time and often requires the household to stay out even after the structure is dry. Ask the mitigation company for moisture readings before you accept a return date, and ask your contractor whether an industrial hygienist's clearance is part of the plan.
